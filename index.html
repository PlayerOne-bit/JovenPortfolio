About

I originally set out to study accounting — that plan didn't work out, and I ended up at STI College instead. It turned out to be the right accident: STI is known for its game development track, which connected straight back to a dream I'd had since elementary school.

Since then the plan has been simple — build a stable career as a software developer, and keep game development alive as a craft I care about on the side. I started with C++ fundamentals before first year even began, moved fast through Java, and by second year was directing a small team through our first game jam. Now in third year, I'm building real, shippable projects instead of just class exercises, with a focus on apps that solve real problems and clean, polished interfaces.
